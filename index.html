Origins

The Ancestral Couples

Long ago, there lived two married couples named Gumalangit and Tendeduata, as well as Tumotoibokol and Tumotoibokat. According to stories passed down from generation to generation, they were the ancestors of the Bolaang Mongondow people.

They lived around Mount Komasaan, in the Bintauna area. Over time, both couples had an ever-growing number of descendants.

Ancestral Pairs Gumalangit & Tendeduata · Tumotoibokol & Tumotoibokat
Early Homeland Around Mount Komasaan, in the Bintauna area

2

Journey

Spreading Toward New Lands

Their children and grandchildren then began to spread out to various regions in search of homes and a better life. Some headed toward the interior, such as Passi, Lolayan, and the area around Kotamobagu.

3

Leadership

The Role of the Bogani

During their journey, they formed community groups. Each group was led by a leader called a Bogani. A Bogani was known as a figure who was brave, wise, and responsible toward their community.

“Bogani” Brave · Wise · Responsible

Guided by steady leadership, each group navigated new territories while keeping their families and shared traditions protected.

4

Unity

Growing Together in Harmony

Little by little, these groups grew and united. They built a life together by upholding unity, courage, and mutual cooperation (gotong royong).

5

Heritage

Living Traditions Today

From the story of their ancestors' journey grew the Bolaang Mongondow community, which up to the present day continues to preserve its customs and culture.

Shared Pillars Unity, courage, and mutual cooperation (gotong royong)
Enduring Legacy Preserving Bolaang Mongondow customs and culture today

6

Reflection

Echoes of the Ancestors

The folklore of Kotamobagu reminds us how courage, wise leadership under a Bogani, and gotong royong shaped a resilient community rooted in Bolaang Mongondow heritage.
